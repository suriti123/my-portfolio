A little about me

Design with purpose, not just decoration.

My background includes IT and UI development, along with hands-on exploration of responsive layouts, visual hierarchy and interface design. I'm building a focused portfolio around landing pages and practical website improvements.

I enjoy looking at a digital experience from a visitor's perspective: what is clear, what is missing, and what could make the next step easier to understand.

Landing page design Visual hierarchy Responsive layouts Figma UI design
My approach
01

Understand

Identify the audience, the business goal and the visitor's needs.

02

Clarify

Organise content and make important information easier to find.

03

Design

Create a considered visual direction with clear calls to action.

04

Refine

Review the layout across screen sizes and improve what needs attention.
